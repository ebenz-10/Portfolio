01 — About

Where the frame meets the code.

Ebenz is a creative designer, videographer and editor based in Kannur, Kerala, currently in the first year of a BSc in Computer Science at St. Joseph's College, Devagiri. The work sits at the intersection of visual storytelling and technical craft — from motion graphics and poster design to the beginnings of frontend development.


As Creative Sub Lead at the Innovation and Entrepreneurship Development Centre (IEDC), Ebenz directs creative strategy for institutional campaigns, and independently runs a freelance editing practice serving individual and business clients across short-form and long-form video.
